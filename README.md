# Sterna Language — UK English Study Guide

**Your language. Your world.**

Sterna Language is a UK-based language-study agency, part of **WES GROUP LTD**. We help international students compare English schools in the United Kingdom and plan the practical details of a study stay. Schools provide the teaching; Sterna provides guidance and separately arranged practical support.

**Official website:** [sternalanguage.com](https://sternalanguage.com/) · **Enquiries:** [info@sternalanguage.com](mailto:info@sternalanguage.com) · [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/sternalanguage/)

## Start with the student's question

- **Which school fits my goal?** [Compare UK schools](https://sternalanguage.com/uk/schools) by city, course goal and published comparison information.
- **What will it cost altogether?** [Understand tuition and additional costs](https://sternalanguage.com/uk/guides/english-course-costs), then request an itemised quote for your dates.
- **Where should I live?** [Compare homestay, residence and independent arrangements](https://sternalanguage.com/uk/accommodation), including travel time, meals and booking conditions.
- **Will I feel at home?** Explore [Sterna's UK support](https://sternalanguage.com/services) and [free-access UK activity ideas](https://sternalanguage.com/uk/life). Confirm meet-up dates, eligibility and extra costs before relying on an activity.

## Scope and accuracy

This directory is not a ranking of the best schools, a promise of availability or evidence that every school is a current Sterna partner. Accreditation belongs to the named school or programme. Group reviews must not be presented as campus-only reviews. Illustrative Sterna scores and fictional design stories are not customer endorsements.

The website offers English, Turkish, Korean, Arabic, Italian, Japanese and Traditional Chinese. Longer translated content is automatic and has not all been professionally reviewed by native speakers. English is the source version.
