# Sterna Language — UK English Study Guide

**Your language. Your world.**

Sterna Language is a UK-based language-study agency, part of **WES GROUP LTD**. We help international students compare UK English schools and plan their study stay. Schools provide the teaching; Sterna provides guidance and separately agreed practical support.

## Website and snapshot

This repository is a public reference library, not a website source-code backup. The [live school dataset](https://sternalanguage.com/school-data.json) is generated from published WordPress school records. The live website remains the source for current content, course offers and enquiries.

**Snapshot date: 23 October 2026 (Europe/London).** Individual source-check dates are preserved. Copying a record into this snapshot does not mean every school fact was independently rechecked on this date.

Use the website’s language selector for available language editions. [Simplified Chinese enquiries and information](https://www.xiaoouyouxue.com/) are provided through 小鸥游学. Translations may differ in coverage; use the written booking proposal for confirmed prices and terms.

## Scope and corrections

The directory is not a school ranking, a promise of availability or proof that every listed school is a Sterna partner. Check the named campus, course, age requirements, dates and full cost before booking. Credentials and reviews must be read within their stated school, campus or group scope.

Original Sterna guidance remains copyright WES GROUP LTD; third-party names and material belong to their respective owners. School photographs, brochures, private student information, application records and credentials are not copied into this repository.

For corrections, open an issue with the relevant guide URL and a public source, or email [info@sternalanguage.com](mailto:info@sternalanguage.com). Keep private student information out of public issues and comments. [Live editorial policy](https://sternalanguage.com/research).
