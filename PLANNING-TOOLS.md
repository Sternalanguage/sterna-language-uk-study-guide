# UK study planning tools

**30 published interactive topics.** Snapshot: 23 October 2026 (Europe/London).

Explore the [live planning hub](https://sternalanguage.com/uk/planning?lang=en) or its [Traditional Chinese edition](https://sternalanguage.com/uk/planning?lang=zh-Hant). Each link below opens the interactive website page; this repository contains the public index, not visitors’ answers or comments.

The hub and topic pages provide a moderated community comment area. Sign in on the website to submit a question for review; keep private booking and contact details out of public comments.

Calculators and checklists organise your own inputs. They do not confirm school availability, eligibility, a booking, an assessment result or an immigration decision. Use the written terms supplied for your actual dates.
