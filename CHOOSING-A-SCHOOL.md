# Choosing an English language school in the UK

The useful question is not “Which school is best for everyone?” It is “Which school fits my learning goal and everyday life?”

## Make a shortlist in six decisions

1. **Goal:** everyday confidence, an IELTS score, professional communication or a longer study stay. Compare [course types](https://sternalanguage.com/uk/courses) before choosing intensity.
2. **Programme eligibility:** your age, current level and dates. A school-level minimum does not apply to every course. A summer centre may serve juniors only; adult learners should confirm the actual programme rather than infer suitability from a school brand.
3. **Teaching time:** compare weekly teaching hours, lesson duration, timetable and class size. Twenty lessons are not automatically twenty hours.
4. **City and commute:** compare the actual teaching address with accommodation. A straight-line distance from a city centre is not the travel time from your room.
5. **Whole-stay cost:** ask for tuition, registration, materials, seasonal supplements, examination fees, accommodation and transfers separately. Match course, duration and year before comparing a discount.
6. **The experience beyond class:** ask the school about its social programme, and separately ask Sterna about local meet-ups. Confirm dates, eligibility and which costs are included.

## Evidence to check

- A campus-specific course page or current brochure.
- The correct teaching address for your dates.
- A relevant accreditation record for the school and programme.
- Review platform, date and scope: campus, whole school or group.
- Written cancellation, payment and accommodation terms.

Create a shortlist from the [UK school directory](https://sternalanguage.com/uk/schools), read the individual school guides and ask Sterna for a comparison around your own goals. A guide is a starting point; your written proposal confirms the actual booking.
