# Comparing the full cost of a UK English study stay

An advertised weekly tuition figure answers only one part of the budget. Use the same course, intensity, duration and year for each school comparison.

| Cost | Questions to ask |
| --- | --- |
| Tuition | What is the course, total teaching time, number of weeks and start date? Is the price per lesson, week or fixed programme? |
| School extras | Are registration, materials, exam fees and seasonal charges included? |
| Accommodation | What room, board, bathroom, dates, deposit and cancellation terms apply? |
| Travel | Are airport transfers optional? What will the daily commute cost? |
| Personal spending | What needs a separate budget for food, activities and other day-to-day costs? |
| Changes | What are payment deadlines and cancellation or date-change conditions? |

## Discount comparisons

A saving should compare matching products. A 20-week afternoon course cannot be compared directly with a one-week morning course or a different year's published rate. Check the price conditions on the school guide and ask for an itemised written quote. Availability and final terms remain subject to confirmation.
