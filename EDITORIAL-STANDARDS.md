# Sterna Language editorial standards

## Who we are

Sterna Language is a UK-based language-study agency, part of WES GROUP LTD. Our focus is helping international students compare English schools in the United Kingdom and plan the practical details of their stay. Teaching is delivered by the chosen school.

## How school information is sourced

School guides combine public campus catalogues, school publications and separately identified evidence. A catalogue import date is not a claim that every fact was independently checked that day. Source links and any verification dates are shown beside the relevant information.

Listed course options, accommodation types and addresses can change. Seasonal centres, junior programmes and different campuses may have different rules. Confirm your teaching building, age eligibility, entry level and course dates in the written booking proposal.

## Prices and offers

A weekly tuition example is specific to a course, duration, year and published conditions. Registration, materials, exams, seasonal supplements, accommodation and travel may be additional. A saving is shown only when the published course and duration can be matched. Final availability and terms are confirmed before booking.

## Reviews and credentials

Published third-party school ratings are labelled with their platform, date and campus or group scope. Illustrative Sterna ratings and fictional stories remain clearly labelled examples and are excluded from rating structured data. A school's accreditation is not Sterna's accreditation; agency or subsidiary credentials are identified separately.

## Local support and activities

Sterna community activities are separate from a school's social programme. Dates, city, eligibility and any extra costs must be confirmed. Free access to a park or museum does not mean transport, food, special exhibitions or a hosted outing are free.

## Translations and corrections

Core navigation and key instructions have edited translations. Longer content uses automatic translation and has not all been professionally reviewed by native speakers. English is the source version. Contact info@sternalanguage.com if a name, address, fee or condition needs correction; rely on your written booking documents for confirmed terms.
